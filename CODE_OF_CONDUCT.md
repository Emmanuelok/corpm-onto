# Code of Conduct

This project adopts a contributor-friendly conduct standard inspired by Contributor Covenant principles.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus critique on ideas, evidence, modelling choices, and implementation details.
- Avoid unsupported allegations, inflammatory language, and personal attacks.
- Be transparent about uncertainty, provenance, and modelling assumptions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Knowingly adding unsupported real-world corruption allegations.
- Misrepresenting synthetic examples as real cases.
- Deliberately breaking validation, tests, or contribution safeguards.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, request changes, or restrict participation when behaviour threatens the safety, trustworthiness, or legal integrity of the repository.
